# Ecumenical Councils

Canonical draft IDs for the 21 ecumenical councils recognized by the Catholic Church, from the First Council of Nicaea (325) to the Second Vatican Council (1962–1965), in sequence order. `#` is the council's position in the Catholic sequence; the ID carries the place-ordinal (which Nicaea, which Lateran). The **Recognized** column records *formal* conciliar reception only — **C** = Catholic, **EO** = Eastern Orthodox, **OO** = Oriental Orthodox, **CE** = Church of the East. The first seven are the corpus shared with the Eastern Orthodox; the first three are shared also with the Oriental Orthodox, the first two with the Church of the East. Doctrinal esteem beyond formal reception (e.g. the authority of the first four across much of historic Protestantism) is discussed in the schema proposal, not encoded here. Popes are given as [CRPDR](../../crpdr) `rp:` IDs. All IDs are drafts pending CETF review ([schema proposal](../docs/schema-proposal.md)).

| 9 | `oec:lateran-i` | First Lateran Council | 1123 | Rome | IT | `rp:callixtus-ii` | Pope Callixtus II | `rp:callixtus-ii` | C | Ratified the Concordat of Worms, ending the Investiture Controversy by distinguishing the spiritual investiture of bishops from their temporal regalia; the first ecumenical council held in the West. |
| 10 | `oec:lateran-ii` | Second Lateran Council | 1139 | Rome | IT | `rp:innocent-ii` | Pope Innocent II | `rp:innocent-ii` | C | Healed the schism of the antipope Anacletus II and enacted disciplinary canons, including the invalidity of marriages attempted by those in major orders (clerical celibacy). |

| 14 | `oec:lyon-ii` | Second Council of Lyon | 1274 | Lyon | FR | `rp:gregory-x` | Pope Gregory X | `rp:gregory-x` | C | Achieved a short-lived reunion with the Greek Church, professed the procession of the Holy Spirit, and enacted the conclave rules for papal elections (Ubi periculum). |
| 15 | `oec:vienne-i` | Council of Vienne | 1311–1312 | Vienne | FR | `rp:clement-v` | Pope Clement V | `rp:clement-v` | C | Suppressed the Knights Templar and condemned errors attributed to the Beguines, Beghards, and the Free Spirit. |
| 16 | `oec:constance-i` | Council of Constance | 1414–1418 | Constance | DE | `rp:gregory-xii` | Antipope John XXIII, at the urging of Emperor Sigismund; convalidated by Pope Gregory XII | `rp:martin-v` | C | Ended the Western Schism of three rival claimants and elected Pope Martin V, and condemned the errors of Wyclif and Hus. |
| 17 | `oec:florence-i` | Council of Florence | 1431–1445 | Florence | IT | `rp:eugene-iv` | Convoked at Basel by Pope Martin V (1431); transferred to Ferrara (1438) and Florence (1439) by Pope Eugene IV | `rp:eugene-iv` | C | Proclaimed reunion with the Greek Church (Laetentur Caeli, 1439) on the Filioque, purgatory, and papal primacy, and further unions with the Armenians, Copts, and others; most proved short-lived. |
| 18 | `oec:lateran-v` | Fifth Lateran Council | 1512–1517 | Rome | IT | `rp:julius-ii` | Pope Julius II | `rp:leo-x` | C | Reaffirmed papal authority over councils against conciliarism, condemned errors denying the immortality of the individual soul, and called for reforms that went largely unrealized on the eve of the Reformation. |
| 19 | `oec:trent-i` | Council of Trent | 1545–1563 | Trent | IT | `rp:paul-iii` | Pope Paul III | `rp:pius-iv` | C | Responded to the Protestant Reformation, defining Scripture and Tradition, original sin and justification, and the seven sacraments, and enacting sweeping disciplinary reform. |
| 20 | `oec:vatican-i` | First Vatican Council | 1869–1870 | Vatican | VA | `rp:pius-ix` | Pope Pius IX | `rp:pius-ix` | C | Defined the primacy and infallibility of the Roman Pontiff (Pastor Aeternus) and taught the harmony of faith and reason (Dei Filius). |
